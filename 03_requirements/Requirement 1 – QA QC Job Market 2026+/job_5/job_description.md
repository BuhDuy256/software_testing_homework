## Job Description

- Control and evaluate the implementation of compliance with prescribed processes of projects according to their standards.
- Control progress, warn and detect problems that may affect the project progress.
- Synthesize, make statistics, analyze and report data according to required standards; presenting risks, problems and recommendations.
- Perform tasks related to process management as assigned by the department leader/head.
- Support projects to comply with the software development process according to the Agile – Scrum model.
- Participate in the development and implementation of CMMi and periodic ISO audit.
- Support training activities; consult and propose to build processes suitable to actual projects.
