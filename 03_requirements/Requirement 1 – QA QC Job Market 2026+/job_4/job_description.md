# QA/QC Engineer (Junior – Middle)

**Work Location:** Ho Chi Minh  
**Level:** Junior, Middle  
**Expiration Date:** 30 Jun, 2026

---

## Job Description

- **Test Execution:** Execute manual test cases to ensure product quality and functionality.
- **Defect Reporting:** Identify, document, and track defects using defect management tools.
- **Test Case Development:** Develop and maintain comprehensive test cases based on project requirements and specifications.
- **Regression Testing:** Conduct regression testing to ensure that new features do not adversely affect existing functionalities.
- **Collaboration:** Work closely with cross-functional teams, including developers, product managers, and other stakeholders, to resolve issues.
- **Documentation:** Maintain accurate documentation of test results, defect reports, and test case modifications.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Participate in process improvement initiatives and contribute to best practices in quality assurance.
- **Training and Development:** Stay updated on industry trends and advancements in quality control methodologies and tools.

---

## Your Skills and Experience

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Logistics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.
- From 2 years of experience in quality control or software testing.
- Knowledge of various types of testing: unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.

### Technical Skills
- Basic understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC) and testing concepts.
- Proficiency in using bug tracking tools (e.g., Jira) and test management tools to organize test cases, track defects, and manage test execution.

### Analytical Skills
- Ability to analyze requirements and identify test scenarios.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Ability to write clear and comprehensive test documentation.

### Soft Skills
- English: Effective communication skills to collaborate with developers, product managers, and other stakeholders.
- Team Player: Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
