## Job Description

Lead a transformation of our current quality assurance practices, being involved in close collaboration with Development, Product Management, and senior business stakeholders, while keeping customer impact at the forefront.

---

## Your Skills and Experience

### Required Qualifications
- 7+ years of experience in software quality assurance, including **3+ years in a QA management role**.
- Proven track record of building and scaling QA teams, including hiring, mentoring, and performance management.
- Hands-on experience writing automated tests in at least one language (JavaScript, Python, Java).
- Proven experience designing or maintaining test automation frameworks and using automation tools (Selenium, Playwright, Postman, and CI/CD pipelines).
- Experience managing an automation team of **5+ engineers**.
- Ability to strategically assess and guide the use of generative AI in QA workflows, even without direct implementation responsibilities.
- Experience implementing QA metrics and driving improvements in efficiency, automation, and product quality.
- Excellent communication skills, both technical and non-technical. English proficiency at upper-intermediate level or higher.
- A person- and process-oriented leader.

### Desired Qualifications
- Experience managing QA for SaaS or enterprise software with complex integrations (ideally involving Microsoft 365).
- Participation in initiatives involving AI agents or co-pilot-style tools to enhance QA workflows.
- Familiarity with backup, storage, or data protection solutions.
- Experience working with cloud storage platforms (e.g., AWS S3, Wasabi, Azure) and virtualization technologies (e.g., VMware, Hyper-V, Proxmox).
